Our thesis

4:1 is an energy problem

The limit in 2026 was not lift. It was carrying the load for the whole course. We will attack power density, efficiency and weight, and film every attempt.

Our aircraft

A gas-electric single-rotor helicopter

Gasoline main rotor

Gasoline holds about 45 times more energy per kg than lithium batteries. That is what a 10 nmi loaded course needs.

Electric tail rotor

No tail driveshaft or gearbox to carry, and fast, precise yaw control.

Every gram counts

We optimise the engine, blades and airframe to be as light as possible.

Proven layout: the 2026 winner was also a single-rotor helicopter, at 3.84:1.

Energy: gasoline ≈ 12.9 kWh/kg vs Li-ion packs ≈ 0.25 kWh/kg. Source for 2026 winner: DroneXL, Aug 10, 2026
